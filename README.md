# Md Asaduzzaman Rasel | Academic & Research Portfolio

Welcome to my academic and research portfolio. This repository showcases my education, research experience, scientific publications, conference presentations, and technology innovation in biophysics and photomedicine.

## About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Biophysics at the University of Massachusetts Boston, working in cancer biophysics and photomedicine. My research focuses on accessible photodynamic therapy (PDT), photodynamic diagnosis (PDD), photosensitizer detection, and low-cost biomedical device development.

## Research Interests

- Photodynamic Therapy (PDT) and Photodynamic Diagnosis (PDD)
- Protoporphyrin IX (PpIX) and Photoprotoporphyrin IX (PPP) photophysics
- Optical spectroscopy and fluorescence detection
- Biomedical optics and low-cost diagnostic devices
- Adaptive dual-wavelength light delivery
- Technology translation and commercialization
